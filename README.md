# Gahai HMS Architecture

Enterprise Architecture & System Design Documentation

---

## Overview

Gahai HMS is an AI-assisted Hospital Management System designed for dental clinics, hospitals, and multi-specialty healthcare organizations.

This repository contains architecture specifications, design documents, workflow definitions, and system planning documentation for the Gahai HMS platform.

The goal of Gahai is to provide a unified healthcare platform that integrates:

* Patient Management
* OPD & IPD
* Dental & Orthodontics
* Implant Management
* Pharmacy Management
* Central Inventory
* Billing & Financial Ledger
* Workflow Automation
* Communication & CRM
* Reporting & Document Management
* Analytics & Business Intelligence
* AI-Assisted Clinical Intelligence
