# Gahai HMS AI & RAG Architecture

## Overview

Gahai HMS is designed as an AI-assisted healthcare platform.

AI is intended to support healthcare professionals, improve operational efficiency, and enhance decision making while maintaining human oversight.

---

## Core Philosophy

Human First

AI Assisted

Audit Driven

Evidence Based

Explainable

---

## AI Objectives

* Reduce administrative burden
* Improve documentation quality
* Enhance operational visibility
* Support clinical workflows
* Improve knowledge retrieval

---

## AI Use Cases

### Clinical Documentation Assistance

Generate structured summaries from clinical records.

### Patient Timeline Summarization

Summarize long patient histories.

### Operational Intelligence

Identify workflow bottlenecks and operational risks.

### Financial Intelligence

Detect anomalies and revenue leakage.

### Inventory Intelligence

Identify shortages, expiries, and purchasing trends.

---

## Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)

The platform will utilize RAG architecture to provide context-aware assistance using:

* Patient Records
* Clinical Notes
* Reports
* Documents
* Internal Protocols
* Knowledge Base Articles

---

## Knowledge Base

The knowledge base may include:

* Clinical Guidelines
* Hospital SOPs
* Treatment Protocols
* Training Material
* Administrative Policies

---

## AI Governance

AI recommendations are advisory.

Human review remains mandatory.

AI systems may not:

* Modify records without approval
* Approve financial transactions
* Make independent clinical decisions
