# Gahai HMS Platform Architecture

## Purpose

Gahai HMS is designed as a unified healthcare platform rather than a collection of disconnected applications.

The system provides a shared platform for:

* Patient Management
* Clinical Operations
* Financial Operations
* Inventory Management
* Communication
* Reporting
* Analytics
* Artificial Intelligence

---

## Architectural Vision

One Patient System

One Financial System

One Inventory System

One Workflow System

One Communication System

One Reporting System

One Analytics Platform

One AI Platform

---

## Core Platform Domains

### Patient Domain

Central source of truth for all patient information.

### Clinical Domain

Manages encounters, treatment plans, procedures, investigations, and clinical records.

### Financial Domain

Manages charges, payments, deposits, refunds, and ledger entries.

### Inventory Domain

Manages pharmacy, inventory, procurement, and assets.

### Communication Domain

Manages calls, SMS, WhatsApp, emails, notifications, and CRM activities.

### Reporting Domain

Manages reports, documents, images, and patient records.

### Analytics Domain

Provides dashboards, KPIs, executive reporting, and operational intelligence.

### AI Domain

Provides clinical intelligence, RAG systems, workflow assistance, and operational analytics.
