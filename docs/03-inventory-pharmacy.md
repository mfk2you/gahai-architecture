# Gahai HMS Inventory & Pharmacy Architecture

## Overview

Gahai separates Pharmacy and Inventory into distinct systems.

---

## Pharmacy

Manages medicine inventory.

Examples:

* Paracetamol
* Amoxicillin
* Ibuprofen

Supports:

* Batch Tracking
* Expiry Tracking
* Drug Sales
* Drug Purchases
* Returns

---

## Inventory

Manages all non-medicine stock.

Examples:

* Cotton
* Gloves
* Syringes
* Dental Materials
* Implants
* Equipment

Supports:

* Procurement
* Stock Transfers
* Department Stores
* Reservations
* Asset Tracking

---

## Core Principle

One Inventory Ledger

One Pharmacy Ledger

Complete Stock Traceability
