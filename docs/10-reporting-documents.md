# Gahai HMS Reporting & Documents Architecture

## Overview

The Reporting Platform provides a unified repository for all patient and operational documents.

---

## Supported Documents

Clinical Documents

* Prescriptions
* Clinical Notes
* Discharge Summaries

Reports

* Laboratory Reports
* Pathology Reports
* Radiology Reports

Financial Documents

* Invoices
* Receipts
* Credit Notes

Administrative Documents

* Consent Forms
* Registration Documents
